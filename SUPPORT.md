saves you time, here's how to give back.

## 🤝 Free ways to support

- ⭐ **Star the repository** on [GitHub](https://github.com/miralabs-tech/standardoc) — visibility helps a lot.
- 💬 **Share feedback & ideas** via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/miralabs-tech/standardoc/issues).

## 💰 Financial support

### OpenCollective

Recurring or one-time donations support core development, language providers,
performance work, and infrastructure costs.

👉 https://opencollective.com/standardoc

### Optional paid companion (maybe, eventually)

The focus stays on the open-source Core. As long as Standardoc has no
cloud/server component that would justify recurring infrastructure, there
is **no reason to offer a subscription** — and none is planned.

If a companion tool ever ships down the road (e.g. a GitBook-style local
doc UI that runs on your machine, no hosting), here's what's locked in:

- **Local-only** — runs on your machine, no cloud, no SaaS dependency.
- **One-time lifetime license** — no subscription, no per-seat.
- **Tooling-style scope** — never gates the OSS Core surface.

Whether it ships at all depends on project adoption and clear user demand.
The Core itself is locked to convert from FSL-1.1-MIT to **plain MIT on
April 26, 2028** regardless.

If you'd like to signal interest in a companion tool, [open a discussion](https://github.com/miralabs-tech/standardoc/discussions).

## 🏢 Commercial support

Available for:

- **Custom language providers** — adding a non-roadmap language for internal use.
- **Paid integration support** — wiring Standardoc into custom internal tooling.
- **Enterprise sponsorship** — funded development of features your org needs prioritized.
- **Sponsored language coverage** — fund the work to bring a language up to first-class support.

Contact via the email in [`Cargo.toml`](crates/standardoc-cli/Cargo.toml) `authors`.

Your support helps:

- Build more language providers (Python, Go, Java, Swift, …)
